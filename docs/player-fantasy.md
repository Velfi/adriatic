# The Fantasy and the Fun

## The fantasy

- You are a daring pilot connecting a community spread across many islands.
- Everyone needs something, and everyone has something to offer.
- You carry letters, medicine, parts, news, and people across the sea.
- Other people give you food, tools, repairs, shelter, knowledge, and help.
- Progress means becoming trusted and needed—not gaining rank or authority.
- The islands do not belong to you. You belong to the islands.

## The fun

- Discover what people need and who can help them.
- Trade favors and combine them into clever journeys.
- Choose your route, protect your cargo, and fly through changing weather.
- Express yourself through skillful flying, risky approaches, and difficult
  arrivals.
- See each journey change something: a motor starts, a shelf is stocked, a
  letter gets a reply, or two people meet.
- Watch help travel through the community and return from unexpected places.
