# Mouse emote: curious head tilt

## Goal

Create a small, appealing curiosity response that layers cleanly over idle.

## Motion brief

Attention snaps gently toward a subject, one ear leads, the head tilts and moves
slightly forward, and the body rises onto the forepaws. A tiny corrective tilt
in the opposite direction gives the action a questioning cadence.

## Work

- Add optional attention target support shared with point and sniff.
- Author notice, primary tilt, corrective tilt, hold, and recovery beats.
- Blend head roll/yaw, neck reach, asymmetric ears, forepaw rise, and tail tip.
- Keep the action usable as a subtle dialogue reaction with configurable hold.
- Add deterministic trigger, captures, and tests for target clamping and recovery.

## Acceptance

- The action reads at normal camera distance without extreme head rotation.
- Ear asymmetry leads the head motion and remains compatible with headgear.
- A long hold remains alive through restrained breathing and ear motion.
