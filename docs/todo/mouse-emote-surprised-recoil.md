# Mouse emote: surprised recoil

## Goal

Create a fast, interruptible surprise reaction for discoveries and near misses.

## Motion brief

The mouse recoils backward with chest compressed, forepaws lifted defensively,
ears sharply raised then spread, and tail stiffened for balance. It freezes for
a beat, checks the source, and cautiously returns to neutral.

## Work

- Author a fast impact response followed by hold, reassessment, and recovery.
- Accept optional source direction and mirror the lateral component accordingly.
- Coordinate spine compression, backward weight shift, defensive paws, ears,
  head focus, and tail stiffness.
- Prevent visual ground sliding while shifting weight away from the source.
- Allow higher-priority locomotion or danger states to interrupt recovery.
- Add deterministic trigger, directional captures, and interruption tests.

## Acceptance

- The first recoil frame reads immediately without teleporting the body.
- Paws and tail do not penetrate the ground or accessories.
- Reactions from left and right are equally natural and deterministic.
