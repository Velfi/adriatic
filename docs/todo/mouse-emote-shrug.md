# Mouse emote: shrug

## Goal

Create a compact “I don’t know” gesture for dialogue and player expression.

## Motion brief

Both forepaws turn outward, shoulders lift, the head draws down and tilts, ears
spread, and the tail gives a small uncertain curl. The pose holds just long
enough to read before relaxing.

## Work

- Author preparation, shoulder lift, open-paw hold, and release curves.
- Add symmetrical paw presentation with slight timing asymmetry.
- Coordinate neck compression, head tilt, ear spread, and tail curl.
- Verify accessory and scarf clearance in the compressed shoulder pose.
- Add deterministic trigger, capture coverage, and timing/cancellation tests.

## Acceptance

- The pose reads as uncertainty rather than cheering or surrender.
- Both paws remain visible from the standard three-quarter camera.
- The neck and shoulders compress without collapsing the body volume.
